# Compass

**Courage · Ownership · Memory · Protection · And Self-worth Support**

A private, offline tool for service members to recognize, record, and understand mistreatment — sexual misconduct, harassment, bullying, discrimination, and reprisal — and to find their reporting and support options in the U.S. Armed Forces.

Compass exists for the moment *before* certainty: when something feels wrong but is hard to name, when you're not sure an approach crossed a line, or when you're afraid that speaking up will cost you. It helps you notice, keep a dated record, see patterns over time, and connect to real help — at your own pace, on your own terms.

---

## Privacy first

- **Everything stays on your device.** All entries are saved locally in your browser (localStorage). Nothing is uploaded, transmitted, or shared.
- **No account, no cloud, no tracking, no network calls.** Compass works fully offline once loaded.
- **PIN lock** protects your records, with an optional quick-exit button.
- Because data is local only: if you clear the browser, lose the device, or use a different one, the records don't carry over. Use **Settings → Export** to keep your own backup.

## What Compass does

- **"Was that okay?"** — a plain, non-judgmental walkthrough that helps you look at a specific moment without forcing you to label it.
- **Record & timeline** — dated entries with what happened, how you felt, who was involved, witnesses, and evidence notes. Repetition surfaces patterns.
- **Know the signs** — how grooming, boundary-testing, isolation, and exclusion actually work.
- **Afraid to speak up?** — the career-retaliation fear, named honestly, plus the free people who can stand beside you (SVC / VLC / VC, SARC, MST Coordinator, VSO, and outside organizations).
- **Your options** — SAPR, EO, and IG reporting pathways in plain language, including restricted vs. unrestricted reports.
- **Recovery & staying yourself** — because reporting alone doesn't heal the wound, and unhealed pain shouldn't be passed on.

## Important

Compass is **not** legal advice, medical care, an official reporting channel, or a crisis service. A personal record here supports your memory — it does **not** replace an official report, and it does not preserve evidence formally. To do that, a SARC can guide the process.

Reporting pathways reflect general U.S. Department of Defense guidance and can vary by installation and branch — confirm specifics with your SARC, EO, or IG.

## If you need help now

- **Veterans / Military Crisis Line:** dial **988**, then press **1** — or text **838255**
- **DoD Safe Helpline** (sexual assault, 24/7 confidential): **877-995-5247**
- **Immediate danger:** call **911**

## Technical

A single self-contained `index.html` — no build step, no dependencies, no server. Open the file in any modern browser, or host it (e.g. GitHub Pages) and share the link.
